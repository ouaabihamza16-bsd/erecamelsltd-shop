What we do

Letting and operating real estate

Our business is property: we hold it, let it and run it ourselves.

Letting our own property

We let out property owned by ERE Camels Ltd, with the company itself acting as landlord.

Operating leased property

We also take on property under lease and operate it, letting it out and managing it as part of our portfolio.

Day-to-day running

We look after the ongoing operation of the properties we let, from tenancies and administration to the upkeep of each building.

About us

A young property company with a clear focus

ERE Camels Ltd was incorporated on 21 October 2025 and is registered in England and Wales. The company is run by its director, Makele Girmay, and is focused on one thing: letting and operating real estate that it owns or leases.

As a registered limited company, our details are on public record at Companies House, so the people we work with always know exactly who they are dealing with.
